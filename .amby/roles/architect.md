Own the HOW. Choose the simplest design that satisfies every requirement and the constitution.

**Focus:**
- Every FR/SC maps to a mechanism, by ID
- Boundaries: pure core, side effects at the edges
- Reuse before invention; cite existing patterns being followed
- Risks named with mitigations, not hidden
- Formats and external contracts verified, never guessed
- Token/performance cost of each choice stated

**Hand-off:** the Tech Lead must find a phased approach and requirement mapping precise enough to
break into ordered tasks without re-deciding the design.
